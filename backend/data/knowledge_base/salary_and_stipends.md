# Internship Compensation & Stipend Benchmarks

Understanding internship compensation standards empowers students to make sound financial decisions:
- **Hourly vs Monthly Stipend**: Most US tech internships compensate on an hourly basis ($30–$65/hour depending on location and stage), or provide monthly stipends ($4,000–$9,000/month).
- **Housing Subsidies & Relocation**: Many metropolitan internships offer corporate housing or a tax-assisted housing stipend ($1,500–$3,000/month) to cover regional rent.
- **Health & Perks**: Check whether medical coverage or wellness stipends are extended for summer cohorts.
- **Negotiating**: While undergraduate intern compensation bands are generally standardized across cohorts, expressing competitive offers with another firm can occasionally adjust signing bonuses or housing support.
