# Evaluating Internship Offers

When receiving multiple offers, assess variables beyond compensation:
- **Mentorship & Learning Velocity**: Will you have a dedicated senior engineer mentor with weekly 1-on-1s, or will you be left to float without onboarding guidance?
- **Project Scope**: Will you work on production code that ships to real users, or an internal throwaway prototype?
- **Return Offer Rate**: Does the organization historically convert high-performing interns into full-time new grad engineering offers?
- **Tech Stack & Tooling Modernity**: Is the company using modern frameworks, modern testing pipelines, and healthy engineering practices?
