# Internship Application FAQ

### When should students apply for summer internships?
Tech recruiting for summer internships kicks off as early as August and September of the prior year, peaking between October and February. Early applications receive higher visibility before cohort allocations fill up.

### How many applications should I submit?
Quality and tailoring trump raw volume. Submitting 20–40 thoughtful, tailored applications with customized cover letters and portfolio links yields significantly higher response rates than blasting hundreds of generic one-click applications.

### Do unpaid internships count?
Legitimate engineering and business internships at for-profit companies must generally be compensated under federal fair labor standards. PlacePilot flags stipends transparently to help students prioritize fair compensation.

### What if I don't meet 100% of the job requirements?
Job postings represent an employer's aspirational wish list. If you meet approximately 60% of the core competencies and demonstrate eagerness and related project experience, you should definitely apply.
