# Professional Interview Etiquette for Students

Mastering professional etiquette ensures your technical capability is received with confidence:
1. **Punctuality**: Log in to virtual interview rooms 3 to 5 minutes prior to the scheduled time. Test your microphone, camera, and network connection beforehand.
2. **Environment & Presence**: Choose a quiet, well-lit environment. Maintain conversational eye contact by looking near the webcam rather than down at notes.
3. **Structured Communication**: Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioral questions. Be concise and emphasize your personal contribution.
4. **Active Inquiry**: Always prepare 3 thoughtful questions for the interviewer (e.g., "What does mentorship look like for interns on this team?", "What is the biggest engineering hurdle the team tackled this quarter?").
5. **Post-Interview Follow-Up**: Send a concise, personalized thank-you email within 24 hours acknowledging specific topics discussed during the interview.
