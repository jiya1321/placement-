# Effective Cold Outreach & Networking Guide

Cold outreach on LinkedIn or via professional email can bypass crowded ATS applicant queues when executed politely:
- **Target the Right Person**: Reach out to university recruiters, campus talent partners, or engineering managers on teams you admire.
- **Keep it Brief (Under 100 words)**: Introduce who you are, what specific aspect of their team's work caught your attention, and link your portfolio or GitHub.
- **Ask for Advice, Not an Immediate Job**: Request a brief 10-minute informational chat to learn about their engineering culture or internship experience.
- **Sample Cold Note**:
  "Hi [Name], I'm a CS sophomore passionate about distributed databases. I followed your team's recent blog post on database sharding and was thoroughly impressed. I'd love to ask two brief questions about how your engineering team structures intern projects if you have 10 minutes this week. Thank you for your time!"
