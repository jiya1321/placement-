# Tailored Cover Letter Essentials

A great cover letter should never read like a dry recitation of your resume:
1. **Varied Openings**: Hook the reader with a genuine anecdote, a high-impact technical triumph, or a direct reaction to an open-source tool built by the company. Avoid opening with "I am writing to express my enthusiastic interest in...".
2. **The "Why You + Why Them" Intersection**: Dedicate one paragraph specifically to what unique problem this company solves and why your specific interests align with their engineering roadmap.
3. **Evidence Over Adjectives**: Instead of calling yourself "a passionate, hard-working problem solver", demonstrate it by referencing how you resolved a tricky caching bug or engineered a clean multi-agent pipeline.
4. **Brevity**: Limit the letter strictly to 3 concise paragraphs (under 300 words). Hiring managers spend less than 40 seconds skimming.
